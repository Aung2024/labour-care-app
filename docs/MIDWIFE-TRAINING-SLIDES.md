# m-MNCH Care — Training Slide Deck Outline

**For trainers:** Copy each slide into PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Keynote.  
**Suggested format:** 16:9 · Large font (24pt+) · One idea per slide · Myanmar speech + English button labels on screen.

**Screenshots:** Use images from `docs/user-manual-assets/` (filenames noted per slide).

**Companion:** [Training guide](./MIDWIFE-DIGITAL-TRAINING-GUIDE.md) · [Myanmar handout](./MIDWIFE-HANDOUT-MYANMAR.md) · [User manual docx](./m-MNCH-Care-User-Manual.docx)

## How to present

- **Slide** = what the room sees  
- **Speaker notes** = what you say (can be in Myanmar)  
- **Demo** = pause deck and do on projector + trainees' phones  
- **⏱** = suggested timing for 2 half-day course
