# m-MNCH pilot-month Ministry briefing — speaker notes

## Evidence basis
- Facility-level live extract dated 14 August 2026.
- Netlify usage screenshots covering 11–18 August 2026.
- Tatkon team feedback from 18 August 2026.
- Figures describe recorded activity, not population coverage or a linked patient cohort.

## Slide 1 — Purpose
Present early evidence of adoption, service recording and implementation learning. Frame this as a pilot improvement discussion, not a final performance assessment.

## Slide 2 — Executive summary
**Say:** The pilot has established real use across 35 facilities. ANC is the dominant workflow. Tatkon carries most of the recorded delivery and high-risk workload. The primary constraint is documentation time and staffing.

**Avoid:** Describing 1,267 records as 1,267 unique people served without confirming deduplication and retrospective entries.

## Slide 3 — Digital use
**Say:** Daily users repeatedly navigate clinical forms, showing active workflow use. The antenatal form is the most-used named page.

**Caveat:** Pageviews are not clinical services; offline PWA activity may be undercounted.

## Slide 4 — Maternal-care footprint
**Say:** 805 ANC clients generated 1,692 service records, averaging 2.1 recorded ANC services per client. Early ANC was recorded for about one-third.

**Interpret carefully:** ANC 4+ and 8+ are affected by the short pilot period, retrospective registration and incomplete documentation.

## Slide 5 — Delivery, PNC and newborn
**Say:** Newborn recording is close to recorded delivery volume, and PNC within 42 days is documented for nearly three-quarters of PNC clients.

**Priority checks:** PNC within 48 hours, LCG completion and zero KMC records need workflow and definition validation. Do not state that KMC was not provided clinically.

## Slide 6 — Township comparison
**Say:** Tatkon records more repeat ANC, high-risk tracking and delivery activity; Pyinmana records higher ANC reach among registered mothers.

**Avoid:** Ranking clinical performance. Differences may be driven by case mix, facility mix, historical data and documentation practices.

## Slide 7 — Facility concentration
**Say:** A few facilities carry a large share of activity. This justifies differentiated workflows and targeted implementation support. Tatkon Township Hospital alone accounts for 27% of registrations.

## Slide 8 — Frontline feedback
**Say:** The barrier is operational fit, not primarily software rejection. Staff need a workflow that protects clinical time. Hospitals need a team-based high-volume pathway; midwives need longitudinal continuity.

## Slide 9 — Data quality
**Say:** The pilot has already identified concrete data-governance actions. The briefing tab is stale by 10 registrations; facility totals are the source used in this deck. Severe anemia and KMC zero values require validation.

## Slide 10 — 30-day actions
Prioritize a small set of changes that reduce burden and improve evidence: hospital fast-track, shared roles, save-later, essential fields, weekly exception review and Dashboard V2 completion.

## Slide 11 — Decisions requested
Ask for approval of a differentiated Tatkon hospital workflow pilot, named facility focal persons, a minimum dataset and a 30-day review.

## Core Ministry message
> The first pilot month demonstrates real frontline adoption and useful clinical-data capture. The next barrier is operational: documentation must fit staffing, patient volume and facility type. The recommended next phase is not simply “more training”; it is workflow redesign, targeted support and stronger indicator governance.
