# m-MNCH Care — Digital Training Guide for Midwives

**Audience:** Pilot midwives (often 35+ years, limited smartphone experience)  
**Trainer use:** Run a 1–2 day hands-on session before or alongside the [m-MNCH Care User Manual](./m-MNCH-Care-User-Manual.docx)  
**Companion docs:** [User flow](./USER-FLOW.md) · [Step-by-step manual](./MIDWIFE-USER-MANUAL.md) · [App sitemap](./APP-SITEMAP.md) · [Myanmar handout](./MIDWIFE-HANDOUT-MYANMAR.md) · [Slide deck outline](./MIDWIFE-TRAINING-SLIDES.md)

---

## How to use this guide

| Section | Who reads it | When |
|---------|--------------|------|
| **Part A — Trainer notes** | You (trainer) | Before and during the session |
| **Part B — Teach the midwives** | Print or project for trainees | During training |
| **Part C — Practice checklist** | Trainees + trainer | Hands-on lab |
| **Part D — One-page handout** | Give to each midwife | Take home after training |

**Teaching style that works for this group**

- Show on a big screen, then let each person **do the same step on their own phone** before moving on.
- Use **Myanmar** for explanations; use **English only for button names** on screen (Login, Save, Sync, etc.).
- One topic at a time. Do not rush through registration + ANC + LCG in one hour.
- Repeat the **three daily habits** many times: *Search first · Save carefully · Sync before you go home*.
- Praise small wins. Fear of “breaking the phone” is the biggest blocker—not lack of intelligence.

# Part A — Trainer notes

## Recommended training plan (2 half-days)

### Half-day 1 — Digital basics + app foundation (3–4 hours)

| Time | Topic | Method |
|------|-------|--------|
| 30 min | Why we use the app; patient privacy | Discussion + stories |
| 45 min | Phone/tablet basics (Section 3) | Demo + each person practices |
| 30 min | Internet, opening the app, login (Sections 4–5) | Demo + login on own device |
| 45 min | Home screen, language, logout (Section 6) | Walk through together |
| 60 min | Search patient → register → consent (Section 7) | **Hands-on with fake patient** |
| 15 min | Recap + homework: open app at home, do not change password yet | |

### Half-day 2 — Clinical workflows + safety (3–4 hours)

| Time | Topic | Method |
|------|-------|--------|
| 30 min | Recap + questions from homework | |
| 45 min | Patient Care Hub + ANC entry (use manual Ch. 7) | Pair practice |
| 45 min | Offline + Sync (Section 8) | Turn off WiFi exercise |
| 30 min | Digital security deep dive (Section 2) | Quiz-style discussion |
| 30 min | When things go wrong (Section 9) | Scenario cards |
| 30 min | End-of-day routine + one-page handout (Part D) | |
| Optional | LCG / newborn / PNC | Use manual chapters 8–10 if time |

**Minimum viable training (1 day only):** Half-day 1 fully + Home → Search → Register → ANC one visit + Sync + Security + Handout.

- [ ] **Internet** (WiFi or mobile data) for first login and account approval.
- [ ] Each midwife has **approved login** (email + password from administrator).

# Part B — What to teach the midwives

## 1. Is it “cyber security”?

**Simple answer for trainees:**

> We are not teaching you to be IT experts. We are teaching **digital security** — how to keep **patient information** and **your account** safe when using a phone or tablet.

“Cyber security” is the big professional word for this. For daily work, use:

| English | Myanmar (for explanation) | Meaning for midwives |
|---------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| Digital security | ဒစ်ဂျစ်တယ် လုံခြုံရေး | Safe use of phone + app |
| Patient data / information | လူနာ အချက်အလက် | Names, phone, clinical records |
| Password | လျှို့ဝှက်စကား (Password) | Key to **your** account only |
| Login / Logout | အကောင့်ဝင် / ထွက် | Open and close your account |
| Shared device | မျှသုံးသော ဖုန်း | Phone used by many staff |

---

## 2. Basic digital security (must teach)

### 2.1 Golden rules — say these aloud until everyone can repeat them

1. **မပေးပါနှင့်** — Never give your password to anyone (not supervisor, not IT, not family).
2. **မမျှဝေပါနှင့်** — Do not share patient details on Facebook, Viber, or personal chat.
3. **ထွက်ပါ** — Log out when you finish, especially on a shared phone.
4. **မကြည့်ပါနှင့်** — Do not open the app for curious browsing; open it only for real patient work.
5. **မဖျက်ပါနှင့်** — Do not delete the app or “clear browser data” without asking your focal person (you can lose unsynced records).
6. **မျှဝေဖုန်း သတိထား** — On shared devices, do **not** tick “Keep me logged in”.

### 2.2 Password rules (keep it practical)

Teach them to create a password they can remember but others cannot guess:

- **Good:** Mix of letters and numbers they know only — e.g. a private word + birth year + symbol (`*` or `#`).
- **Bad:** `123456`, `password`, their phone number, `midwife1`.
- **Bad:** Writing password on a sticker on the phone cover where everyone can see.

**If they forget the password:** Contact administrator/focal person. **Do not** ask a colleague to “use their login” for you — that breaks accountability and privacy rules.

**Account lockout:** After too many wrong passwords, the account locks. Wait or call admin. Do not keep guessing.

### 2.3 Protecting patient privacy (clinical + digital)

| Situation | Safe behaviour |
|-----------|----------------|
| Someone asks “What is in the app about Mrs X?” | Only discuss if they are authorised clinical staff for that patient; never gossip. |
| Family member asks to see records on your phone | Follow facility policy; usually only staff with role-based access. |
| Phone screen visible in public | Angle screen away; lock phone when not in use. |
| Taking photos of screens | Do not send patient screens to personal chat groups. |
| List view shows hidden phone (e.g. `09 XXX XXX 678`) | That is intentional masking — full number is inside the patient record when needed. |

### 2.4 Device physical security

- Keep phone in **pocket or bag**, not left on charge unattended in public areas.
- Set a **screen lock** (PIN or pattern) on the phone itself — separate from app password.
- Report **lost or stolen phone** to supervisor **immediately** so account can be disabled.
- Do not install random apps or click unknown links while logged into MNCH (reduces virus/scam risk).

### 2.5 Scams and tricks (simple version)

Tell them:

- Ministry, Jhpiego, or admin will **never** ask for your password by phone call or message.
- If a message says “click here to fix your account”, **do not click** — show the focal person first.
- Only use the **official app link** you were given in training.

## 3. Smartphone & tablet basics (for digitally inexperienced users)

*Skip only if every trainee already uses a smartphone confidently.*

### 3.1 Parts of the screen

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│  Status bar (time, battery) │  ← Battery low = save work & sync soon
├─────────────────────────────┤
│                             │
│     App content area        │  ← Forms, lists, buttons
│                             │
├─────────────────────────────┤
│  Home indicator / buttons   │  ← Phone navigation
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.2 Essential actions

| Action | How | Myanmar hint |
|--------|-----|--------------|
| **Tap** | Light touch with one finger | ခေါက်လိုက်ပါ — like pressing a button |
| **Scroll** | Drag finger up/down | အပေါ်/အောက် ဆွဲပါ |
| **Type** | Tap in box; keyboard appears | အကွက်ထဲ နှိပ်ပြီး ရိုက်ပါ |
| **Back** | Phone back arrow or app ← | နောက်သို့ — does not always mean “cancel save” |
| **Save / Submit** | Green or primary button at bottom | သိမ်းမယ် — wait for success message |

**Common fear:** “I will break it.” Reassure them: tapping wrong place does not break the phone; worst case they go back and try again.

### 3.3 Keyboard tips

- If Myanmar keyboard appears but field needs **numbers** (age, BP), switch to **123** numeric keyboard.
- If they cannot find `@` for email: hold `0` or switch to symbols (`#+=`).
- **Capital letters** for password matter — `A` and `a` are different.

### 3.4 Opening the app

**Option A — Installed app (PWA / “Add to Home Screen”)**

1. Find **MNCH** icon on home screen.
2. Tap once. Wait for loading.

**Option B — Browser link**

1. Open **Chrome** or **Safari**.
2. Tap the address bar; type or select the link from bookmarks.
3. Bookmark it: “MNCH App” for next time.

**Trainer demo:** Show both ways on Android and iPhone if your group mixes devices.

## 4. Internet: WiFi and mobile data

### 4.1 Simple explanation

- **WiFi** — internet through clinic/hospital wireless (often free for staff).
- **Mobile data** — internet through SIM card (costs money; uses package).

App needs internet for: **first login**, **account approval**, **sync**, **some forms** (LCG, delivery notes, immunization, transfers, many reports).

### 4.2 How to know if internet works

- Open another app or browser page.
- In MNCH, if pages load very slowly or show errors, try moving to better signal or WiFi.

### 4.3 Offline banner

When internet is gone, app may show an **offline** message. Teach:

> You can still do **some** work offline, but not everything. Always **Sync** when internet returns.

(Details in Section 8.)

---

## 5. Login and first-time setup

### Step-by-step (match user manual Ch. 3)

1. Open app → **Login** screen.
2. Type **Email** (exactly as admin gave you).
3. Type **Password** (careful with capitals).
4. **Keep me logged in** — tick **only on personal device** used only by you; needed for offline login.
5. Tap **Login**.
6. First time: read **Provider Consent** → **I Accept**.
7. You should see **Home** screen with your name/email and cards.

**Trainer tip:** Stand behind each person for first successful login. Failed login in front of peers is embarrassing and causes avoidance.

## 6. What is m-MNCH Care? (brief overview)

### 6.1 One-sentence explanation

> **m-MNCH Care** is a Ministry of Health maternal and child health app that records a mother and baby’s care journey in one place — from pregnancy (ANC) through labour (LCG), newborn care, and after birth (PNC), plus immunization and reports.

### 6.2 What the app is NOT

- Not a replacement for clinical judgment.
- Not a chat app or social media.
- Not automatic without you entering data — **if you do not save, it is not recorded**.

### 6.3 Main idea: one patient, one journey

```
Register or find patient
        ↓
 Patient Care Hub (centre of everything)
        ↓
   ┌────┴────┬──────────┬──────────┐
  ANC     Labour    Newborn      PNC
 (ကိုယ်ဝန်)  (မွေးဖွား)  (မွေးကင်း)  (မွေးပြီး)
```

**Mother pathway:** Register → ANC visits → Labour (LCG) → Delivery notes → PNC  
**Baby pathway:** Often created automatically at delivery → Newborn care → Immunization

### 6.4 Home screen — two cards they will use every day

| Card | When to use |
|------|-------------|
| **Patient Registration** | Brand-new patient not in system |
| **Select Patient for Care** | **Always try this first** — existing patient |

Other cards (Transfers, Dashboard, CME, High Risk, Scoreboard) — teach as “second week” topics unless role needs them immediately.

### 6.5 Language

- **ENG / MM** on Home — interface language only.
- Clinical entries may still need Myanmar patient names as usual.

## 7. Core clinical habits (not optional)

These prevent the most common real-world errors.

### 7.1 Search before register

```
Patient arrives
    → Home → Select Patient for Care
    → Search name or phone
    → Found? Open hub. Do NOT register again.
    → Not found? Then Patient Registration.
```

**Why:** Duplicate records split history; same phone number is **blocked online**.

### 7.2 Mommy vs Baby filter

- **Mommy** — pregnant woman / mother care (ANC, LCG, PNC).
- **Baby** — child’s immunization and newborn follow-up.

If search finds nothing, app may hint: “Try Baby tab” or “Try Mommy tab”.

### 7.3 Patient Care Hub

After selecting a patient, the **hub** shows the right modules for that person. This is where they live during a visit — not random pages from Home.

### 7.4 Consent before save (registration)

Registration form → **Consent page** → then data is really saved. Teach them not to leave before consent step.

### 7.5 Save means wait

After **Save** / **Submit**:

- Wait for success message or next screen.
- Do not close app immediately.
- If internet is weak, wait longer — do not tap Save five times fast.

### 7.6 Baby after delivery

**Preferred:** Mother’s hub → **Delivery Notes** → baby record created automatically.  
**Manual baby registration** — only for babies not born through your facility’s delivery workflow.

## 8. Offline work and Sync (critical for rural sites)

### 8.1 What works offline (simplified)

| Usually OK offline | Usually needs internet |
|--------------------|-------------------------|
| Some registration (with limitations) | Labour care (LCG) full workflow |
| Some ANC / PNC entry | Delivery notes |
| Viewing cached pages | Immunization |
| | Transfers |
| | Most reports & dashboard |

Exact behaviour can vary — teach: **if it fails, note it and sync when online**.

### 8.2 Offline duplicate check gap

**Important teaching point:** When offline, phone duplicate check may not run. Same phone registered twice is possible offline — causes problems later. **Search list carefully even offline.**

### 8.3 Daily Sync routine (end of every working day)

1. Connect to internet (WiFi or data).
2. Go to **Home**.
3. If you see pending offline records, tap **Sync**.
4. Wait until success message.
5. Then logout (if shared device) or lock phone.

**Mnemonic in Myanmar:**  
*“ရုံးဆင်းမီ — Sync လုပ်”* (Before leaving duty — Sync.)

### 8.4 Never do these without focal person

- Uninstall app
- Clear browser data / cache for Chrome
- Factory reset phone
- Change phone without syncing old phone first

---

## 9. When things go wrong — calm responses

| Problem | What midwife should do |
|---------|------------------------|
| Wrong password | Stop guessing; call focal person |
| Account locked | Call admin |
| Blank patient list | Check filters (Mommy/Baby, status); check internet; call if still empty |
| Save does nothing | Check red required fields `*`; check internet; try once more |
| Wrong patient opened | **Change Patient** from hub |
| App slow | Close other apps; move to better signal; refresh |
| Sync failed | Keep internet stable; go Home → Sync again; **do not delete app** |
| Screen confusing | Note what they tapped; screenshot if possible; tell focal person |

**Emotional coaching:** Mistakes are normal in week 1. Important thing is to **ask early**, not hide problems.

## 10. Other topics you should teach

Beyond security and app overview, these dramatically improve adoption:

### 10.1 End-of-day and end-of-week routine

1. Finish or save visits.
2. **Sync**.
3. Logout on shared devices.
4. Charge phone for tomorrow.

### 10.2 Charging and battery

- Low battery during labour recording is a real risk. Charge at nurses’ station; carry power bank if facility allows.

### 10.3 Who to call for what

| Issue | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Login, password, account approval | Admin / focal person |
| Clinical “what to enter” | Senior midwife / supervisor |
| App bug or broken screen | Focal person + Feedback form |
| Lost phone | Supervisor immediately |

Write **one phone number** on the handout.

### 10.4 Data quality (short)

- Correct **phone number** and **LMP/EDD** — affects whole pathway.
- Fill **required fields** — reports and dashboards depend on them.
- Record **same day** when possible.

### 10.5 Professional use only

Phone is a **work tool** during duty. Personal calls and social media between patients → more mistakes and privacy risk.

### 10.6 Motivation

- App supports **better continuity** when patient transfers.
- **Leaderboard / CME** are for learning and motivation — quality matters more than speed.
- Their feedback during pilot **improves the app for all Myanmar midwives**.

### 10.7 Supervised practice period

Recommend **2 weeks** where each midwife has a “buddy” (younger colleague or champion user) before working alone with the app.

# Part C — Hands-on practice checklist

Trainer signs off when trainee completes each item on their own device.

## Digital basics

- [ ] Unlock phone; open MNCH app from icon or bookmark
- [ ] Switch WiFi or mobile data on/off and explain difference
- [ ] Tap, scroll, and type in a text field

## Account

- [ ] Login with own email and password
- [ ] Accept provider consent (if shown)
- [ ] Switch language ENG ↔ MM on Home
- [ ] Logout and login again

## Patients

- [ ] Open **Select Patient for Care** and search by name
- [ ] Switch **Mommy** / **Baby** filter
- [ ] Register **practice mother** (or trainer-approved dummy)
- [ ] Complete **patient consent** step
- [ ] Open **Patient Care Hub** for that patient

## Clinical (minimum)

- [ ] Start one **ANC visit** and save (or reach save screen in demo mode)
- [ ] Open **Overall report** or ANC report and view data

## Offline & security

- [ ] Explain the 6 golden security rules without reading
- [ ] Demonstrate **Sync** on Home (or explain if no pending data)
- [ ] Explain when to use **Keep me logged in** vs logout

**Pass criteria:** Can login alone, find or register a patient, open hub, save one visit, and describe sync + password rules.

## Quick trainer FAQ

**Q: Should I call it cyber security?**  
A: You can say “digital security” or “patient information safety.” Mention “cyber security” once as the formal name — no need to dwell on it.

**Q: How much IT detail?**  
A: Almost none. Focus on behaviour: password, logout, sync, don’t delete app.

**Q: English-only manual with Myanmar trainees?**  
A: Teach from Myanmar docx; use this guide for session structure; demonstrate on live app in MM language.

**Q: What if they cannot read well?**  
A: Pair with a champion user; use more demonstration and repeated practice; audio Q&A; picture cards from manual screenshots (`docs/user-manual-assets/`).

**Q: What to assign as homework?**  
A: Login once at home; open manual chapter 4 (Home screen); practice search on one real patient with supervisor present.
