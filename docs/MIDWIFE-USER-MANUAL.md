## 1. Introduction

The **m-MNCH Care** app helps you manage the full care pathway for mothers and babies: from **antenatal** through **labour**, **immediate newborn**, **postpartum**, and **baby care**. As a midwife, you can:

- Register new patients and avoid duplicates
- Record antenatal visits, labour care, newborn care, and postpartum visits
- View a single **Patient Care Hub** for each woman and her baby
- Generate reports and view your activity on the leaderboard
- Use the app on phone or tablet (Android/iOS) or in a web browser

This manual walks you through every main screen and workflow, with placeholders for screenshots and guidance on how to use the app effectively.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Logging In

1. Open the app (or go to the app URL in your browser).
2. You will see the **Login** screen with the app name and logo.
   - **[Screenshot: Login screen – email and password fields, Login button, link to registration]**
3. Enter the **email** and **password** given to you by your administrator.
4. Tap or click **Login**.
5. If your credentials are correct, you will be taken to the **Home** screen. If not, you will see an error message (e.g. wrong password or account locked after too many failed attempts).
   - **[Screenshot: Login screen with error message – wrong password or account locked]**

**Recommendation:** Keep your password private and do not share your login. If you forget your password, contact your administrator.

---

### 2.2 First-Time Provider Consent

The first time you log in (or when consent is required again), you may be asked to accept the **Provider Consent** before using the app.

1. After login, if consent is needed, you will be redirected to the **Provider Consent** page.
   - **[Screenshot: Provider Consent page – consent text and Accept / View full policy buttons]**
2. Read the consent text (use “View full policy” if available).
3. Tap or click **I Accept** (or equivalent) to continue.
4. You will then be taken to the **Home** screen.

**Note:** Until you accept, you cannot use the rest of the app. This is required for compliance and data use.

---

### 2.3 Home Screen Overview

The **Home** screen is your main entry point. It shows your email, role (e.g. Midwife), and several cards (modules).

1. After login (and consent, if applicable), the **Home** screen appears.
   - **[Screenshot: Home screen – user info at top, language switcher, Settings and Logout, grid of cards]**
2. You will see at least these cards:
   - **Patient Registration** – register a new patient
   - **Select Patient for Care** – open the patient list and choose a patient
   - **Analytics Dashboard** – view reports and charts
   - **CME - Continuous Medical Learning** (Midwife only) – learning modules
   - **Scoreboard** – leaderboard rankings
3. Use the **language** switcher (e.g. ENG / MM) if you want to change the interface language.
4. Use **Settings** (gear icon) to manage your profile and preferences; use **Logout** to sign out.
   - **[Screenshot: Home screen – Patient Registration and Select Patient cards highlighted]**

**Recommendation:** For daily work, the two cards you will use most are **Patient Registration** and **Select Patient for Care**. Start from Home each time you begin a session.

## 3. Patient Registration

Use this flow when you need to **register a new pregnant woman** in the system.

### 3.1 Open Registration

1. From **Home**, tap or click the **Patient Registration** card (or “Register New Patient”).
2. You will be taken to the **Patient Registration** page (patient-enhanced).
   - **[Screenshot: Patient Registration page – full form with sections visible]**

### 3.2 Fill In Patient Details

The form is divided into sections. Complete all required fields (usually marked with *).

1. **Personal / basic information** – e.g. name, date of birth, phone number, address.
   - **[Screenshot: Registration form – Personal Information section]**
2. **Pregnancy-related information** – e.g. LMP (Last Menstrual Period), EDD (Expected Due Date), parity (G and P), risk level if applicable.
   - **[Screenshot: Registration form – Pregnancy / parity / LMP / EDD section]**
3. **Other sections** – e.g. demographic, contact, facility/township if present.
   - **[Screenshot: Registration form – remaining sections]**

**Tip:** Enter the phone number correctly. The app checks for **duplicate patients** by phone (and name/age). If a possible duplicate is found, you will be warned before saving.

### 3.3 Duplicate Check and Save

1. After filling the form, tap or click **Register** (or **Save** / **Submit**).
2. If the app finds a **possible duplicate** (same or similar phone/name), a message or list will appear. Review it and either choose the existing patient or confirm that this is a new patient.
   - **[Screenshot: Duplicate patient warning – list of possible matches and option to continue or go back]**
3. If there is no duplicate (or you confirm new), the patient is saved and you may see a success message or be taken to the patient list or Care Hub.
   - **[Screenshot: Registration success message or redirect to list/hub]**

**Recommendation:** Always check duplicate warnings. Registering the same woman twice can lead to split records and confusion. If in doubt, select the existing patient and add a new visit instead of creating a new registration.

## 4. Selecting a Patient & Patient Care Hub

To give care, you must **select a patient**. From the patient list you open that patient’s **Patient Care Hub**, which is the central place for all her care (antenatal, labour, newborn, postpartum, baby).

### 4.1 Open the Patient List

1. From **Home**, tap or click **Select Patient for Care** (or “Select Patient”).
2. The **Select Patient** (list) page opens, showing your patients (as a midwife, you see patients linked to you).
   - **[Screenshot: Patient list – search box, filter chips, list of patient cards]**

### 4.2 Search and Filter

1. Use the **search box** to type the patient’s name, phone, or patient ID.
   - **[Screenshot: Patient list – search box with text entered]**
2. Use **filter chips** (e.g. All, Antenatal, Labour, Postnatal, Baby, Transferred) to narrow the list by status.
   - **[Screenshot: Patient list – one or more filter chips selected]**
3. You can switch between **list** and **card** view if the app offers a view toggle.
   - **[Screenshot: Patient list – card view vs list view]**

### 4.3 Select a Patient and Open Care Hub

1. Find the patient in the list and tap **Select** (or the patient card/row).
2. You are taken to the **Patient Care Hub** for that patient.
   - **[Screenshot: Patient Care Hub – header with patient name, status, key details, and care cards]**

### 4.4 Patient Care Hub Overview

The **Patient Care Hub** shows:

- **Patient summary** – name, ID, status (e.g. Antenatal, Labour, Postnatal), risk, EDD, etc.
- **Care cards** – shortcuts to:
  - Antenatal Care
  - Labour Care (setup, monitoring, entry)
  - Immediate Newborn Care
  - Newborn / Baby Care (e.g. vaccine schedule)
  - Postpartum Care
  - Summary / Overall report
- **Change Patient** – returns you to the patient list to choose another woman.

   - **[Screenshot: Patient Care Hub – care cards grid]**
   - **[Screenshot: Patient Care Hub – patient info bar with Change Patient button]**

**Recommendation:** Get into the habit of always opening the **Patient Care Hub** first, then choosing the right care card. This keeps the correct patient in context and reduces errors.

## 5. Antenatal Care

You can record **antenatal visits** and related data from the Care Hub or from the dedicated Antenatal Care area.

### 5.1 Open Antenatal Care

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Antenatal Care** (or go to **Home → Select Patient →** select patient, then from the hub tap Antenatal).
2. You may land on **Antenatal Care** (antenatal-care) where you can select the patient (if not already set) and see options: **Add ANC Visit**, **ANC Visits List**, **Antenatal Report**, etc.
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal Care page – patient selector and module cards]**
3. If the patient is already selected from the hub, you may go straight to the ANC visit list or form.

### 5.2 Add an ANC Visit (Antenatal Form)

1. Choose **Add ANC Visit** or open the **antenatal form** (antenatal-form) for the selected patient.
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal form – header with patient name and form sections]**
2. Fill in the visit details (e.g. visit number, date, gestational age, blood pressure, weight, fundal height, presentation, fetal heart, urine protein, haemoglobin, risk factors, advice given).
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal form – first section of clinical fields]**
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal form – second section or table of visits]**
3. If the form shows a **table of existing ANC visits**, you can add a new row or new visit and save.
4. Tap **Save** (or **Submit**). A success message or return to the list confirms the visit was recorded.
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal form – Save button and success message]**

**Tip:** The app may validate **EDD and LMP** (e.g. ~280 days apart). If you get a validation message, correct the dates before saving.

### 5.3 Antenatal Tests and Reports

1. From the Antenatal Care area, use **Antenatal Tests** (or tests list) to record or view lab/test results.
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal tests list or form]**
2. Use **Antenatal Report** (or similar) to view or print a summary of antenatal care for the patient.
   - **[Screenshot: Antenatal report view]**

**Recommendation:** Record each ANC visit soon after the consultation. Complete key fields (BP, weight, gestational age, risk) so that labour and postpartum teams have an up-to-date picture.

## 6. Labour Care

Labour care includes **setting up** a labour record, **recording labour progress** (e.g. partograph), and **outcomes**.

### 6.1 Start Labour Care (Setup)

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Labour Care** (or the labour-related action).
2. If no labour record exists, you will be guided to **Labour Care Setup** (labour-care-setup). The app may open it automatically or show a “Start labour” option.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour Care Setup – patient summary and form to start labour]**
3. Enter the required setup information (e.g. date/time of onset, facility, any initial findings) and tap **Start** or **Save**.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour Care Setup – form filled and Start button]**
4. After setup, you can open **Labour Care Entry** (or monitoring) to record progress.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour Care Entry or monitoring screen – partograph or labour form]**

### 6.2 Record Labour Progress (Labour Care Entry)

1. Open **Labour Care Entry** from the Care Hub (e.g. “Labour care” or “Record labour”) for the selected patient.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour Care Entry – main screen with partograph or data entry]**
2. Enter cervical dilatation, contractions, fetal heart rate, descent, and other observations at the required intervals. Save or auto-save as you go.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour Care Entry – partograph or table with data]**
3. When labour ends, record the **outcome** (e.g. vaginal delivery, caesarean, transfer, other) on the same page or via a dedicated outcome screen.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour outcome selection or form]**

### 6.3 Labour Protocols and Emergencies

1. From the Labour Care area (or hub), you may have links to **Labour Protocols** and **Labour Emergencies** for reference.
   - **[Screenshot: Labour protocols or emergencies page]**
2. Use these as reference only; record actual care and outcomes in the labour care entry and outcome screens.

**Recommendation:** Complete labour setup as soon as the woman is in labour, and update the partograph regularly so that delays or complications are visible and can be acted on.

## 7. Immediate Newborn Care

Right after birth, you can record **immediate newborn care** (e.g. resuscitation, Apgar, drying, warming, first feed).

### 7.1 Open Immediate Newborn Care

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Immediate Newborn Care** (or similar).
2. The **Immediate Newborn Care** form opens for the current patient (mother). You may need to confirm or select the baby if multiple births.
   - **[Screenshot: Immediate Newborn Care – form or list of babies]**

### 7.2 Fill and Save

1. Enter the required data (e.g. time of birth, sex, weight, Apgar, resuscitation steps, vitamin K, first breastfeed).
   - **[Screenshot: Immediate Newborn Care form – key fields]**
2. Tap **Save**. You may then be able to add another baby or return to the Care Hub.
   - **[Screenshot: Immediate Newborn Care – Save and success]**

**Recommendation:** Record immediate newborn care as soon as possible after delivery so that the record is accurate and the baby is linked correctly to the mother.

## 8. Newborn & Baby Care

After the immediate period, **newborn care** and **baby care** (e.g. growth, vaccinations) are recorded on dedicated screens.

### 8.1 Newborn Care Page

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Newborn Care** (or open it from the newborn/baby card).
2. The **Newborn Care** page (newborn-care-page) opens. Select the baby if there is more than one, then enter or edit newborn care data (e.g. feeding, weight, checks).
   - **[Screenshot: Newborn Care page – form or list]**
3. Save your entries.
   - **[Screenshot: Newborn Care – saved record or list]**

### 8.2 Baby Care & Vaccine Schedule

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Baby Care** or **Vaccine Schedule** (or open vaccine-schedule with the patient ID).
   - **[Screenshot: Vaccine schedule or Baby care page]**
2. View or record vaccinations and baby growth/checks as per your facility’s workflow.
   - **[Screenshot: Vaccine schedule – list or form with dates]**

**Recommendation:** Keep baby records under the correct mother in the Care Hub. Use the vaccine schedule to avoid missed immunisations.

## 9. Postpartum Care

**Postpartum (postnatal) visits** are recorded from the Care Hub or the Postpartum Care area.

### 9.1 Open Postpartum Care

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Postpartum Care** (or Postnatal).
2. The **Postnatal Care** (postpartum-care) page opens. You may see a list of visits or a selector for the patient.
   - **[Screenshot: Postpartum Care – list or patient selector]**

### 9.2 Add a Postpartum Visit

1. Tap **Add visit** (or **New visit** / **Postpartum form**).
2. Fill in the visit details (e.g. date, maternal condition, bleeding, blood pressure, breastfeeding, baby check, family planning, advice).
   - **[Screenshot: Postpartum form – main sections]**
3. Save. The visit appears in the postpartum list and in the patient’s timeline.
   - **[Screenshot: Postpartum visit list after adding]**

**Recommendation:** Schedule and record postpartum visits according to national guidelines (e.g. 24–48 hours, 7 days, 6 weeks) and document any complications or referrals.

## 10. Other Workflows

### 10.1 Edit Patient Details

1. From the **Patient List**, tap **Edit** on a patient card (or open the patient and find **Edit patient**).
2. You are taken to **Edit Patient** (edit-patient). Update only the fields that need changing (e.g. phone, address, risk status).
   - **[Screenshot: Edit Patient – form with updated fields]**
3. Tap **Update** (or **Save**). Changes are saved and you can return to the list or Care Hub.
   - **[Screenshot: Edit Patient – Update button and success]**

**Note:** As a midwife, you can usually only edit patients you are allowed to see (your own or your facility’s). You may not be able to change some fields (e.g. role-specific restrictions).

---

### 10.2 Patient Transfer

1. When a woman is **transferred** to another facility, open **Transfer** (transfer) from the Care Hub or menu (if available for your role).
   - **[Screenshot: Transfer page – patient and transfer details form]**
2. Enter transfer date, reason, from/to facility, and any handover notes. Save.
   - **[Screenshot: Transfer form – filled and Submit]**
3. The patient’s status may change to “Transferred” so that she no longer appears in your active list (depending on filters).

**Recommendation:** Complete the transfer record as soon as the transfer is decided so that the receiving facility and reports are accurate.

---

### 10.3 Patient Consent

When you need to record **informed consent** from the patient (e.g. for data use or a procedure):

1. Open **Patient Consent** (patient-consent) from the Care Hub or from the relevant care screen when prompted.
   - **[Screenshot: Patient consent form – consent text and signature/accept]**
2. Ensure the patient (or guardian) has been informed, then record that consent was given (e.g. tick box and/or signature). Save.
   - **[Screenshot: Patient consent – recorded and saved]**

**Recommendation:** Obtain and record consent before procedures or data use as per your facility and national policy.

### 10.4 Summary and Overall Report

1. From the **Patient Care Hub**, tap **Summary** or **Overall report** (e.g. summary-view, newsummary, or overall-patient-report).
   - **[Screenshot: Summary or overall report – one-page view of ANC, labour, newborn, PNC]**
2. Use this view to quickly see the full journey (antenatal → labour → newborn → postpartum) and to share or print for handover.
   - **[Screenshot: Summary – print or share option if available]**

## 14. Guidelines & Recommendations

### 14.1 Daily Workflow

- **Start with Home** → **Select Patient** → **Patient Care Hub**, then choose the right care type (ANC, labour, newborn, PNC).
- **Register new patients** only when sure they are not already in the system; always check **duplicate** warnings.
- **Record visits and events** on the same day when possible (antenatal, labour, immediate newborn, postpartum).
- **Use the Summary / Overall report** for handover and to verify that the full pathway is complete.

### 14.2 Data Quality

- Enter **LMP and EDD** correctly; the app validates consistency. Update if the woman’s dates change.
- Complete **required fields** in each form so reports and dashboards are meaningful.
- Use **patient consent** and **provider consent** as required by your facility and national policy.
- **Edit patient** only when necessary; avoid changing identifiers (e.g. name, DOB) unless correcting a mistake.

### 14.3 Security & Privacy

- **Do not share** your login or password.
- **Log out** when leaving the device.
- **Sensitive data masking**: In list views, phone numbers may be partially hidden (e.g. 09 XXX XXX 678). In the patient’s own record you can see full details when needed.
- If the app is used on **shared devices**, clear or lock the device after use as per your facility policy.

### 14.4 Connectivity & Offline

- The app works best with **internet**. On **weak or no connection**, some pages may load slowly or fail to save. Try again when the connection is better.
- If your organisation has enabled **offline mode** (e.g. on Android), data entered offline will sync when you are back online. Ensure you sync before switching devices or logging out.
- If you see **“Connection” or “Sync”** messages, wait for sync to finish before closing the app.

### 14.5 When to Ask for Help

- **Login problems:** Wrong password, account locked → contact your administrator.
- **Missing patients or wrong list:** Your role may restrict which patients you see (e.g. only your own). If you believe a patient should appear, contact support or admin.
- **Duplicate warning:** If you are sure the woman is new but the app suggests a duplicate, contact support with the patient IDs so they can check.
- **Broken or confusing screen:** Note the screen name (or take a screenshot) and describe what you did; send via the **Feedback form** or to your focal person.

## 15. Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| **Cannot log in** | Check email and password. If locked after many failed attempts, wait or contact admin to unlock. |
| **Provider consent appears every time** | Clear browser cache/cookies or re-accept consent. If it persists, contact support. |
| **Patient list empty** | Confirm you are logged in and that you have patients assigned to you. Try removing filters and search. |
| **Save button does nothing** | Check required fields (often marked with *). Check internet. Try again; if it still fails, note the screen and report. |
| **Wrong patient in Care Hub** | Tap **Change Patient** and select the correct one from the list. |
| **Duplicate warning when I know she’s new** | Review the suggested matches; if none is the same woman, you can usually proceed with “Register as new”. If the app blocks you, contact support. |
| **App slow or not loading** | Check internet. Close other apps. Refresh the page (web) or restart the app (mobile). |
| **Missing CME or Leaderboard** | These are role-based. Midwives should see CME and Scoreboard. If you don’t, contact admin to check your role. |
| **Language not changing** | Use the language switcher on Home (e.g. ENG/MM). If some text stays in one language, report it via feedback. |
