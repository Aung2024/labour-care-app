## 1) Background and Rationale

Under the previous consultancy term (Nov 2025 - Jun 2026), the platform evolved from an LCG-focused web app into a broader maternal-newborn digital care system, including ANC, Labour/LCG, PNC, Newborn, Lab records, patient tracking, dashboards, and offline-first support.

As deployment use increases, the next phase should focus on:
- Clinical safety and rapid identification of high-risk cases
- Consistent field adoption (including NPT midwives and township-level users)
- Supervisory visibility and measurable program performance
- Ongoing optimization for low-connectivity and Android field workflows

This extension will consolidate current gains and move the platform to operational maturity.

---

## 2) Objective of the Extension

To strengthen the Labour Care digital platform into a high-adoption, high-safety implementation package by combining:
- Feature enhancements (risk automation, reminders, analytics, data quality controls), and
- Structured field support (training, rollout supervision, and post-training technical assistance).

---

## 3) Scope of Work

### Workstream A: Product Enhancements
1. **High-Risk Intelligence Upgrade**
   - Enhance rule-based auto-detection using ANC clinical findings and registration factors
   - Harmonize risk classification across HRT, Patient Care Hub, and dashboard views
   - Add explainable risk reasons for clinical transparency

2. **Action and Escalation Workflow**
   - Improve follow-up action cycle (called, visited, referred, medication, counseling, resolved)
   - Add escalation indicators for unresolved high-risk cases beyond threshold days
   - Include supervisory visibility on unresolved backlog by user/township

3. **Notification and Reminder Features**
   - In-app notification improvements (risk due, follow-up overdue, unresolved alerts)
   - Optional FCM notification design and integration readiness
   - Reminder logic for ANC/PNC follow-up continuity

4. **Data Quality and Integrity Controls**
   - Missing/invalid data checks for critical fields
   - Consistency checks across modules (status progression and timeline logic)
   - Data issue flags for supervisor review

5. **Dashboard and Monitoring Improvements**
   - Add operational KPIs (response time, unresolved high-risk age, referral pipeline)
   - Provide township/region-level summaries for management review
   - Improve export/report formats for program meetings

### Workstream B: Training and Capacity Building
1. **Training of Trainers (ToT)**
   - Prepare core trainers at central/project level
   - Standardized curriculum for practical case-based use

2. **Field Training Rollout**
   - Conduct training sessions for NPT midwives and selected township users
   - Include practical workflows: registration -> ANC -> high-risk follow-up -> referral -> PNC/newborn

3. **Refresher and Reinforcement**
   - Follow-up refresher sessions after major releases
   - Provide concise quick-reference guides (English/Myanmar)

### Workstream C: Deployment Support and Governance
1. **Release and Hypercare Support**
   - Structured release windows with immediate support after each rollout
   - Ticket triage and prioritized bug-fix cycles

2. **Program Coordination and Reporting**
   - Biweekly technical/program check-ins
   - Monthly progress reports with milestone tracking and risk log
