👉 Facility တွင်သာ ဆက်လက် စစ်ဆေးကုသရန် အကြံပြုပါသည်။ ⚠️ Danger Alert Pitting edema (swelling of the legs with indentation on pressure) is present.
👉 This may be an early warning sign of Pre-eclampsia / Eclampsia.
👉 Blood pressure monitoring, urine protein testing, and further clinical assessment are required.
👉 Continuation of care and management at a health facility is strongly recommended.
