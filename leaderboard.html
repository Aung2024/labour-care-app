How Scores Are Calculated

Each milestone is awarded once per patient on the date it is completed and credited to the provider who recorded it.
+1 Patient Registration
1 point for each registered patient
+2 Complete Patient Registration
2 points for each registered patient with all information filled up
+1, +1, +1 ANC Visits
1 point for each patient with at least 1 ANC visit. Extra 1 point for each patient with 4 or more ANC visits. Extra 1 point for each patient with 8 or more ANC visits.
+2 Complete ANC Form
2 points for each patient with at least 1 ANC visit and have all ANC form filled up
+1 LCG Completed
1 point for each LCG completed patient
+1 PNC Visit
1 point for each patient with at least 1 PNC visit
+1 Complete PNC Info
1 point for each patient with at least 1 PNC visit and have all PNC info filled up
+1 Lab Test
1 point for each patient with at least 1 lab test saved
+1 Completed Delivery Note
1 point when a clinically complete delivery note is saved
+1 Immediate Newborn Care
1 point when immediate newborn care is completed
+1 KMC Enrolment
1 point for the first KMC Yes decision for each baby/patient record
+1 Newborn Care
1 point for each patient with newborn care page saved
+1 Transfer Record
1 point for each patient with transfer record saved
